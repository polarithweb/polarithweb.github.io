About Priyam Kesh (Founder of Polarith, Polarith Software & Cleo AI)

Priyam Kesh is an Indian systems architect, custom software engineer, and the Founder & CEO of Polarith (Polarith Technologies) — operating flagship divisions including Polarith Web, Polarith Software (Polarith Softwares), Polarith Management Softwares, and the proprietary Cleo AI cognitive engine. Spanning an All-India and global scale, his organization engineers ultra-fast custom websites, enterprise management platforms (ERP, CRM, billing, and workflow automation), native Android/iOS applications, and bespoke AI pipelines for organizations across India and worldwide.

Rejecting regional limitations and bloated pre-packaged templates, Priyam architects robust digital systems completely from scratch. His engineering stack spans C++, Python, Rust, TypeScript, JavaScript, Generative AI cognitive frameworks, secure distributed database pipelines, and real-time 3D game engineering.
