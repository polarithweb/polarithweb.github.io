Polarith School Management Software Suite

A complete one-time ownership digital transformation system for schools. Includes a branded School ERP App in your school's own name, integrated or standalone LMS, paperless QR Library with a complimentary Computer and Barcode/QR Scanner, ~1,000 multilingual e-books, automated doorway RFID/Fingerprint/Face attendance, and a free official school website — 100% owned by the school with zero yearly subscription.

Google Play Store (Android) · Optional App Store (iOS) · Library PC Included · Local Hard Disk + Google Cloud
Ownership Terms
01.

School ERP App (In Your School's Own Name)

Published in your school's official name on the Google Play Store (Android) and optionally on the Apple App Store (iOS), connecting office staff, teachers, students, and parents in real time.

  • Student Attendance: Complete daily and monthly student attendance and absence records verifiable anytime.
  • Class Routine & Holidays: Daily grade-wise timetable and the complete annual holiday calendar.
  • Fees Collection: Transparent digital fees ledger tracking paid installments and pending dues cleanly.
  • Teacher Management: Daily teacher attendance logs alongside accurate monthly teacher salary calculation.
  • Notices & Circulars: Instant mobile announcements, exam alerts, and urgent updates for parents, teachers, and students.

Learning Management System (LMS)

Run the LMS integrated directly inside your School ERP App or as a separate dedicated app. The school and teachers keep 100% control over classes, videos, and tests.

  • Live Online Classes: Teachers conduct scheduled online classes directly from the school platform.
  • Chapter-wise Offline Recorded Videos: Organized subject and chapter video archive for revision or catching up on missed classes.
  • School Notes & PDFs: Teacher-authored notes, worksheets, and PDF study files delivered straight to students.
  • Online Tests: Chapter-wise assessments and full-syllabus online exams with structured digital evaluation.
03.

Paperless QR Library Management System

Eliminate handwritten library registers forever. Includes a complimentary Computer, Barcode/QR Scanner, and unique QR Code Stickers for every book — permanently owned by the school.

  • Complimentary Hardware Owned by School: Dedicated library computer, barcode/QR scanner, and custom book QR stickers transferred 100% to the school.
  • Instant Student-to-Book Entry: Scan the book QR and student ID card QR to register borrowing automatically.
  • Exact Issue & Return Records: Instant lookup of which student borrowed which book, issue date, and due return date.
  • Zero Register Errors: Prevents lost book disputes and eliminates manual ledger writing completely.
04.

Digital Library (e-Books)

Provide students with approximately 1,000 curated e-books accessible anytime on mobile phones, tablets, and computers.

  • EPUB & PDF Formats: Clean, readable digital books formatted for comfortable study on every screen.
  • Multilingual Collection: Includes e-books in English, Bengali, Hindi, plus any additional language requested by the school.
  • Unlimited Simultaneous Reading: Multiple students can read the exact same book at the same time with zero physical copy shortages.
05.

Digital Student Attendance & RFID / Biometric Hardware

Fast, accurate entrance attendance without classroom roll-call delays. For card scanning, schools choose either a QR/RFID Sticker on existing ID cards or a Full Digital ID Card.

  • 3 Main Doorway Scanners (RFID / Barcode / QR): Three scanners mounted at the main entrance so students check in quickly with zero crowding.
  • Biometric Fingerprint Scanner: Reliable cardless fingerprint terminal recording student arrival and departure automatically.
  • Contactless Face Recognition (Face ID): Modern touchless attendance camera recognizing students instantaneously as they walk in.
06.

Complimentary Official School Website

A custom-coded official school website built by Polarith Web is included completely free of charge with your school suite.

  • Campus Identity & History: Dedicated pages for school heritage, principal message, faculty, and academic achievements.
  • Admissions & Urgent Notices: Clear admission steps, inquiry contacts, and instant public circulars for parents.
  • SEO-Optimized & Fast: Hand-coded for mobile speed and top local Google visibility.
Included Paperless Library Architecture

How the 4-Step QR Library Workflow Operates

STEP 01
Book & Student QR Setup

Every book receives a unique QR sticker, and every student ID card carries their personal QR code.

STEP 02
Instant Dual Scan

No handwritten registers. Scan the book's QR sticker and immediately scan the student's ID QR code.

STEP 03
Automatic Registration

The software automatically creates the student's Library Book Entry without manual typing.

STEP 04
Exact Return Tracking

The school's owned computer tracks exact borrower names, issue dates, and return dates permanently.
